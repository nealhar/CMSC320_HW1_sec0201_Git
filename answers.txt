Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git provides an easy way for version control and an easy method to revert changes if something breaks.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science could be applied to marketing, for instance data science could be used to see user data and push them ads that they are more likely to click on.


Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging is good for multiple branches and preserves the branches whereas rebasing rewrites the commit history and produces a linear history.
